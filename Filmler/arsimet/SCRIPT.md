# Script: Arşimet: Gerçek mi, Efsane mi?

## Angle
- **Logline:** Seven famous stories about Archimedes put to the viewer as a quiz (Doğru · Kısmen · Efsane) and checked against the oldest sources; the least known story turns out the truest.
- **Turn:** Before: the famous stories are the real Archimedes. After: they are mostly half true, and the true one was hidden under a prayer book, found in İstanbul.
- **Why this angle:** the user's brief (a quiz of claims, easy to follow); the order rises from the familiar (bath) to the unknown (palimpsest), and the İstanbul promise at 0:20 keeps Turkish viewers to the end.
- **Tells kept:** the eight quiz questions (the line after each claim, marked 'soru') are real questions with three answers on screen and a countdown: the film's format, asked by the user.
- **Score:** 2 DOĞRU (pençe, kitap) · 4 KISMEN (evreka, dayanak, burgu, son söz) · 1 EFSANE (aynalar).
